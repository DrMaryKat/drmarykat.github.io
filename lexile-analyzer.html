About these numbers. Official Lexile measures are proprietary to MetaMetrics and come only from their licensed analyzer. This tool reports a Lexile-equivalent estimate: it averages six published readability formulas into a consensus grade level, then maps that grade onto the Common Core text complexity bands. Treat the estimate as a range (about plus or minus 100L), and pair it with a qualitative review of meaning, structure, language, and knowledge demands before making placement decisions.
